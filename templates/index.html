Limitations

Read before trusting the numbers

  • Simulated customers approximate real ones; a clone is built from a handful of lines.
  • Text only: no audio timing, interruptions or prosody in the replays.
  • The judge is an LLM. Every model here is Gemini, so self-preference bias is reduced (judge ≠ bot model) but not eliminated. Human agreement from judge-check is the real validity evidence.
